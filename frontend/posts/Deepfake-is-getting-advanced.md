We also know how generative AI is getting more crazier, and it is good but it is being used for huge scams. Getting scam is easy in this era; now deepfakes are more advanced than before. We all might know that there were video calls from a place that done by a deepfake, which pretends to be a family member and it asks money for emergency and it was not 'AI LIKE`, it was natural in voice and video; some phones had advanced AI features to detact it (mostly chinese brands). It was good but now scamers and hackers are using higher models to generate more realistic deepfake.

Recently, SINGAPORE - INTERPOL launched a public awareness campaign, which shows risk of AI deepfake scams; a fortune's post said in the title :-

> Scammers used AI to clone her daughter’s voice and fake a kidnapping. A voice expert says every family now needs a safe word


We can understand how dangerous it is becoming right now! Be careful and God bless!
