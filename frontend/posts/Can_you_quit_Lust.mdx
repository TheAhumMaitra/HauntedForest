# What harm can Lust do TO YOU?

According to Saint Thomas Aquinas :-

_1_. **Blindness of mind** (caecitas mentis): Sexual pleasure can interfere with one's ability to recognise what is genuinely good.

_2_. **Rashness (praecipitatio)**: Acting without properly considering the consequences.

_3_. **Thoughtlessness (inconsideratio)**: Failing to reflect carefully before making decisions.

_4_. **Inconstancy (inconstantia)**: Difficulty remaining faithful to a rational decision.

_5_. **Self-love (amor sui)**: Seeking one's own pleasure in a disordered way.

_6_. **Hatred of God (odium Dei)**: Resenting divine commandments because they restrict the pleasure one desires.

_7_. **Love of this world (affectus praesentis saeculi)**: Becoming excessively attached to worldly pleasures.

_8_. **Despair of the future world (desperatio futuri saeculi)**: Losing concern for spiritual goods and eternal happiness.

## B : What does "regulating" mean?

Imagine that you experience a desire or attraction. ## C : How do you develop chastity?

_1_. **Practise self-control**: Do not automatically follow every impulse.

_2_.**Develop good habits**: Repeated good choices gradually strengthen virtue.

_3_.**Use reason**: Consider whether your actions are genuinely good.

_4_.**Avoid occasions of sin**: Establish sensible boundaries around things that encourage disordered desires.

_5_.**Seek God's grace**: Pray for strength and spiritual growth.
