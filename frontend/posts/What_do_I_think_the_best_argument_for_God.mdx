There are many arguments for to conclude that there is a God. I always loved the **fine tuning** argument to prove God, my beloved. Fine tuning is best evidence of a supreme deity. According to Trent Horn (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trent_Horn, https://www.catholic.com/profile/trent-horn) from The council of Trent and Catholic Answers (https://www.catholic.com/magazine/online-edition/the-fine-tuning-argument-for-god); the argument goes like :-

_P1_. **The universe possesses finely tuned physical constants and initial conditions that allow intelligent life to exist**.

_P2_. **This is due to necessity, chance, or design**.

_P3_. **It was not due to necessity or chance**.

### Conclusion

_Therefore, the fine-tuning of the universe is the work of a designer_.


Th term _constants_ according to science are letters in a specific equation, which is unchangeable (same from the beginning)


My favorite part is that life would have not existed is the most valid and rational answer, the probability of life existing was low and somehow it seems that life is existed because someone wants so; which can only be conclude to God, a supreme deity; my beloved. It seems to me that the universe was fine tuned for the **existence of life**. 

Now this is the second argument I love that most, _**argument from motion**_ by Aristotle and discussed by favourite philosopher - Saint Thomas Aquinas. And not to mention the most nice and soft argument is _**morality argument**_, which is said by Saint Augestine of Hippo; that morality is objective and it can only come from God. 

So if I make a list, it would be :-
