Oh God thy refugee, how much love you can to me?
Thy love is like the sweetness of a child's laugh.
Thou do not forsake me but I leave you; Thy mother loves me like I am her child.
Thou who is with the world, thou who came.
Thou never leaves me, thy never says "I can't with you".
Thy love is the greatest, yet I do not understand you; I need to find Thou.

Oh, thy refugee; how much do you love me like I worth it?
I owe you nothing, God; my beloved.
Thou is with me, my beloved and my strongest strength of all time;
Thou who always help me to the faith, love you!

Thou is the greatest love I ever find, thou never forget a wicked. 
Thou is the refugee of all time.
